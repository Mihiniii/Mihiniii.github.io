PayHere sandbox checkout

Payment gateway integration

PayHere Payment Demo

A small shop that takes card payments through PayHere in sandbox mode, built to learn how a payment gateway works end to end.

  • The payment hash and the price are made on the server, so the merchant secret stays hidden and the amount can't be changed in the browser.
  • PayHere's payment notification is checked for its signature, merchant ID and amount before an order is marked paid.
  • Orders are saved as pending, then updated from the notification. ngrok gives the notify URL a public address while running on localhost.
